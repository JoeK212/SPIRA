This is Fig. 13.3–13.6's twist operation: a fixed base plane B, a vertical axis A, and every horizontal slice at height z rotated by α(z). Straight vertical edges become helices; a flat wall through A becomes a helicoid. Try α_max = 90° and 180° to reproduce the two right-hand figures on p.456, or push past 360° to see multiple full turns like the Turning Torso.
